What this is

Náströnd is the shore of corpses in Hel, where the dead wash up. It is also a private server running the Desert of Flames era of EverQuest II — the game as it existed roughly twenty years ago, before the expansions stacked up and the world got quietly rebuilt around them.

You do not need an original account, a subscription, or anything you once owned. The launcher downloads the client, points it here, and starts it. There is no store, no donation link, and no way to spend money on this; it is one machine in a house, run for people who already know each other.

The realm

Era Desert of Flames (2005)
Address Set for you by the launcher — there is nothing to type in.
Who can play Invite only. If you are reading this, you are invited.
Population A handful of people. Expect to know everyone online.
Progression Curated and capped, with some things changed from the original. Details are still being settled and will be announced in game.
Character wipes Possible while things are still being built. You will get warning first.
Cost None, ever.

News

  • The realm is up and the launcher works on Windows and Linux. Grab it from the install page and shout if anything breaks.

Something not working?

If the server list appears but character select hangs, the realm is down rather than broken — check the badge at the top of this page. Everything else worth knowing is on the help page.
